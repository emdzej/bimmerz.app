# NavX support: privacy

The **Support** button in the [NavX web app](https://navx.bimmerz.app) sends your report to our support service
([ALIAS](https://getalias.support)). This page says what it keeps and what you can do about it. The rest of the
web app sends nothing anywhere: your discs, navboot and flash stay in your browser.

## What we keep when you ask for help

When you send a report from Bimmerz NavX, we keep:
- what you wrote;
- a picture of the emulator's screen, if you tick it. You can black out parts of it before sending.
  Pictures lose their hidden details when they reach us;
- the details NavX adds about what it was doing:
  - where you were in the app (a tool, or the emulator);
  - whether the emulator ran the emulated OS or the emulated board;
  - the software's name and version;
  - the emulator's state (powered on or off, how long it ran, the flash's software version);
- the emulator's console (its last lines), only if you tick it. It never includes your files;
- the version of NavX;
- our answers.

Your discs, navboot, flash and voices are never sent. Only pictures can be attached.

## Who you are to us

Nobody, by name. The first time NavX talks to our support service, the service gives your browser an anonymous id
and a secret. They aren't made from anything about you, your device or your account anywhere. The id is NavX's
alone: if you use another product with the same support service, it gets a different id, and the two can't be
linked. We don't ask for, and don't keep, your name, email, phone number or account, unless you give an email
(below).

## How long we keep it

- A report: 365 days after it was resolved or closed.
- Everything of your id: 730 days after NavX last talked to the support service.

Both are deleted automatically.

## Your copy, and erasing it

Under **Support › Messages** you can:
- download everything we keep about your id, as a file;
- erase all of it at once. Your id, its reports and pictures, and any email you gave are deleted, for good, at
  once.

## Who sees it

Only the people who answer NavX's support, signed in with their own account, see it. Everything they do
(answering, blocking, erasing) is logged. We use your reports only to help you and to fix NavX. We never sell
them or use them for advertising.

## An email, only if you choose to give one

You may give an email address with a report, so we can answer you by email. Support works the same without it.

If you give one, we keep the address, that it's an email, and when you gave it. We use it only to answer your
reports to NavX, never for news, offers or anything else, and it isn't shared with any other product.

- **You're no longer anonymous to us.** The people who answer NavX's support can see the address. Every time one
  of them opens it, that's logged.
- **We don't check it.** We send no mail to confirm the address, and it's shown to support as unverified.
- **Withdrawing it.** Under **Support › Messages**, at any time. It's deleted at once (not hidden, deleted). It's
  also deleted when you erase your data, and when your id is deleted after 730 days without use.
- **Why we may keep it.** Only because you agreed by giving it to us (consent: GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).
  Withdrawing it doesn't affect the answers we sent before.

If we write to you from our own mailbox, those mails are kept as that mailbox keeps mail.
