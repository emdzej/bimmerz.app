# Bimmerz support: privacy

Some Bimmerz tools have a **Support** button that sends your report to our support service
([ALIAS](https://getalias.support)). This page says what it keeps and what you can do about it. Everything else
in those tools stays on your device: your files are never sent.

## Which tools use it

| Tool | Where | What it adds to a report |
|---|---|---|
| [NavX](/tools/navx/) web app | the **Support** button at the top of [tools.navx.bimmerz.app](https://tools.navx.bimmerz.app) | where you were in the app (a tool, or the emulator); whether the emulator ran the emulated OS or the emulated board; the software's name and version; the emulator's state (powered on or off, how long it ran, the flash's software version). Only if you tick them: the emulator's console (its last lines) and a picture of its screen. Never your discs, navboot, flash or voices. |
| NavX product site | the **Support** button and the **Join the beta** form on [navx.bimmerz.app](https://navx.bimmerz.app) | the page you were on and the topic. The beta form also sends what you fill in — which product you'd like to try, your car, its monitor and software version — and needs an email, kept only with your consent, so we can send you the beta. |
| NavX SDK docs | the **Feedback** button on [sdk.navx.bimmerz.app](https://sdk.navx.bimmerz.app) | the page you were on and the topic. Nothing else. |

Each tool is its own product at the support service, with its own anonymous ids (below); the three NavX sites share one. This page is updated
when another tool gets a Support button.

## What we keep when you ask for help

When you send a report from a tool, we keep:
- what you wrote;
- the pictures you attached, if any. You can black out parts of a picture before sending it. Pictures lose
  their hidden details when they reach us. Only pictures can be attached;
- the details the tool adds (see the table above);
- the version of the tool;
- our answers.

## Who you are to us

Nobody, by name. The first time a tool talks to our support service, the service gives your browser an anonymous id
and a secret. They aren't made from anything about you, your device or your account anywhere. The id is that tool's
alone: if you use another tool or product with the same support service, it gets a different id, and the two can't be
linked. We don't ask for, and don't keep, your name, email, phone number or account, unless you give an email
(below).

## How long we keep it

- A report: 365 days after it was resolved or closed.
- Everything of your id: 730 days after the tool last talked to the support service.

Both are deleted automatically.

## Your copy, and erasing it

In the tool's **Support › Messages** you can:
- download everything we keep about your id, as a file;
- erase all of it at once. Your id, its reports and pictures, and any email you gave are deleted, for good, at
  once.

## Who sees it

Only the people who answer the tool's support, signed in with their own account, see it. Everything they do
(answering, blocking, erasing) is logged. We use your reports only to help you and to fix the tool. We never sell
them or use them for advertising.

## An email, only if you choose to give one

You may give an email address with a report, so we can answer you by email. Support works the same without it.

If you give one, we keep the address, that it's an email, and when you gave it. We use it only to answer your
reports to that tool, never for news, offers or anything else, and it isn't shared with any other tool or product.

- **You're no longer anonymous to us.** The people who answer the tool's support can see the address. Every time one
  of them opens it, that's logged.
- **We don't check it.** We send no mail to confirm the address, and it's shown to support as unverified.
- **Withdrawing it.** In the tool's **Support › Messages**, at any time. It's deleted at once (not hidden, deleted). It's
  also deleted when you erase your data, and when your id is deleted after 730 days without use.
- **Why we may keep it.** Only because you agreed by giving it to us (consent: GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).
  Withdrawing it doesn't affect the answers we sent before.

If we write to you from our own mailbox, those mails are kept as that mailbox keeps mail.
